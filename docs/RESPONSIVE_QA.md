## Acceptance criteria

- No unintended horizontal scrolling on the whole page.
- Long tables may scroll inside their own wrapper without stretching the page.
- Inputs and buttons remain visible, readable and usable.
- Navigation remains accessible and does not overlap page content.
- Background image loads without a 404 or broken-image error.

This checklist is intentionally left unchecked until the pages are visually verified in a browser.
